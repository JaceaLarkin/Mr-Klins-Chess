How to play

Play chess: select a piece, then choose a highlighted legal square. Pawns promote to a queen automatically. The two central pawns on each side are Mimics (✦).

Only a moved Mimic has a 20% chance to detonate. A blast removes it and any other non-king pieces in the eight surrounding squares. After a blast, a separate 1 in 50 chance ends the game with the Mimic mover as the winner.

A Mimic moves and captures one square in any direction. Moves that leave your king in check are illegal. If a player is in check with no legal moves, they lose by checkmate. Kings survive blasts.
