How to play

Play chess: select a piece, then choose a highlighted legal square. Pawns promote to a queen automatically. The two central pawns on each side are Mimics (✦).

After every move, there’s a 20% chance the moved piece detonates. It and every piece in the eight surrounding squares are removed, regardless of color.

A Mimic moves and captures one square in any direction. Moves that leave your king in check are illegal; a blast can still destroy a king. If a king is caught in a blast, that side loses.
